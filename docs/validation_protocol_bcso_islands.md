# Validation protocol I (BCSO flood points and service islands), fixed 2026-10-24 BEFORE any BCSO point was geocoded, any island polygon was made, or any consequence value was compared with any BCSO point

Data: `data/bcso_florence/bcso_FloodRoads_2018.pdf` (legend of known flood points, 20 points) and `data/bcso_florence/wallmaps_flood_islands.pdf` (service-island wall map, 6 pastel fills), both received from the Brunswick County Sheriff's Office (sha256 in `data/bcso_florence/SHA256SUMS.txt`). Before this file was written, only the tool inventory, the file names and the column names of the tables used below were looked at. No point was located, no polygon was drawn, and no `people_affected` value was joined to anything. A sha256 of this file is printed at the top of the report. If a test fails it is reported as failed; no re-run with changed thresholds, radii, sets, seeds or island definitions. **One run.**

**No NWM, recurrence-flow or hazard input is used anywhere in this protocol.** Closures are chosen by the BCSO points (or at random for the baseline), not by any flow. The test asks only whether the road-network mechanism (close a crossing, re-route, count who is cut off or slowed) ranks and separates places the way the Sheriff's Office's own pre-storm flood maps do. It is not a forecast, not a closure probability and not a test of the hazard layer.

## A. Inputs (fixed)
- Crossings: `data/phase0/crossings_v2.parquet` (all 2,920 rows, lat/lon, `county`, `road_carried`, `feature_name`, `nwm_name`, `osm_waterway_type`), closure segment of each crossing = `snap_seg` in `data/phase1b/crossing_snap.parquet` (a crossing without `snap_seg` cannot be closed and is reported as such).
- Consequence: `data/export/consequence_ranking.csv` (`people_affected`, `consequence_status`, `graph_segment_id`; computed from the network alone, one segment closed at a time). **Assessed** = `consequence_status == "assessed"`. Brunswick = `county == "Brunswick"` in `crossings_v2.parquet`.
- Network and engine: `src/pipeline/02_routing/engine2.py` (`Net`, `solve`, `inputs`), graph `data/phase1b/graph_segments.parquet`; origins = 2020 Census block centroids `data/phase1b/origins_snapped.parquet` (rows with `snap_m < 1500`, as in the engine, `pop20` as weight); targets = `hospital_ED_acute` nodes (ED hospitals, non-behavioral) and `fire_station` nodes, exactly as `engine2.inputs()` defines them. Travel time is the graph's normal-condition `time_s`.
- CRS for distances: NC State Plane NAD83 (feet) EPSG:2264; distances are reported in metres.

## B. BCSO points (step 2)
1. The 20 points are transcribed from the legend of `bcso_FloodRoads_2018.pdf` into `config/bcso_flood_points.csv` (columns: id, road, route_no, stream, then method, matched crossing and distance). Nothing else is added or removed; a legend line that cannot be read is kept as a row and marked unreadable.
2. Location method, in this order, logged per point: **M1** the OSM intersection of the named road and the named waterway (OSM road and waterway layers `data/phase0/osm_roads.parquet`, `osm_waterways.parquet`, plus the graph segments and NWM reaches in `data/`), then the nearest crossing in `crossings_v2` to that intersection. **M2** (only if M1 finds no intersection): the crossing in Brunswick County whose `road_carried`, `feature_name` or `nwm_name` contains both the road/route token and the stream token (case-insensitive), nearest to the road's geometry; labelled "name-match".
3. **Matching radius 300 m** from the geocoded intersection to the crossing (M1). If several crossings lie within 300 m, prefer the one whose road/stream names agree, else the nearest. Points with no crossing within 300 m are **unmatched and reported by id and reason, never dropped silently**. The matched set P is the unique crossings matched by at least one point.
4. Secondary (no pass/fail): every result below is also reported with P restricted to M1-matched points only.

## C. Islands (step 3)
- The wall map is rendered at 150 dpi and georeferenced to EPSG:2264 with 4 to 6 ground control points at road intersections identifiable on both the map and the OSM road graph; the fit is a least-squares affine (or projective if the map is visibly distorted) transform. **The georeference is accepted only if the GCP RMSE is at most 100 m**; otherwise the island test I1 is not run and is reported as "not run: georeference RMSE above limit" (I2 is unaffected).
- The six pastel fills are classified by RGB (nearest of six legend colours; white, grey and line work are background). Each class is polygonized; polygons smaller than 0.25 km2 are dropped as annotation. A PNG overlay on the road graph is checked by eye before any test runs; the check can only reject the georeference (above), never change a polygon to make a result better.
- Census block centroids are assigned to an island by point-in-polygon. Blocks in no polygon are excluded from I1 and counted. Islands with fewer than 10 blocks in Brunswick are excluded from I1 and listed.

## D. Tests (both pre-declared; none dropped if it fails)
**I2, ranking (primary).** Unit = unique assessed Brunswick crossing. Positives = P, restricted to assessed crossings. Controls = all other assessed Brunswick crossings. Statistic = AUC of `people_affected` = P(pos > ctl) + 0.5 P(tie). CI = 2000 bootstrap resamples of **crossing clusters** (cluster = `graph_segment_id`, so crossings that share a closed segment move together), percentile method, seed 20261024. **Pass** if the CI lower bound > 0.5.

**I1, separation of islands (primary).** All crossings in P with a `snap_seg` are closed **simultaneously** in `engine2` (`Net.csr(closed)`). For every Brunswick block baseline-reachable to both targets, delta_ED and delta_fire = (closed travel time - baseline travel time) / 60 minutes to the nearest ED hospital and nearest fire station; a block with no path after closure is "lost" for that target (delta = inf). For a pair of blocks (i, j) and each target, separation s = inf if exactly one of the two is lost (a reachability break), 0 if both are lost, else |delta_i - delta_j|; the pair's separation is max(s_ED, s_fire). Pairs: 200,000 draws (seed 20261024) of two distinct blocks with probability proportional to `pop20` each, then split into **cross-island** (different islands) and **same-island** (same island), blocks outside the kept islands excluded. Statistic = AUC = P(s_cross > s_same) + 0.5 P(tie) (inf is larger than any finite value; inf vs inf is a tie). CI = 2000 bootstrap resamples of **islands** (island as the cluster; a pair is weighted by the multiplicities of its two islands), percentile method, seed 20261024. With about six islands the CI will be wide; that is reported, not fixed. **Pass** if the CI lower bound > 0.5.
**Random-closure baseline (reported, no pass/fail).** 200 draws (seed 20261025) of 20 crossings drawn at random from the assessed Brunswick crossings, closed simultaneously, the same pair sample and islands, the same AUC. Report the real AUC's percentile among the 200 and one-sided p = (1 + number of draws with AUC >= real) / 201.

## E. Reported with the verdict table
Counts: legend points, located, matched (M1, M2), unmatched; matched crossings with and without a closable segment; Brunswick blocks, kept blocks, excluded blocks; pairs per class; per-island blocks and population; people lost to ED and fire, and added minutes (median, 90th percentile, max) with all matched points closed; the cross-island/same-island AUC by target (ED only, fire only) as descriptive extras.

## F. What results license
Pass = the road-network mechanism ranks the Sheriff's Office's known flood-prone crossings above other Brunswick crossings by people affected (I2) and the simultaneous closure of those crossings separates the areas the Sheriff's Office drew as service islands more than it separates blocks within an island (I1). It remains one county, hand-drawn operational plans, and pre-storm known flood points, not confirmed closures; it says nothing about forecast skill, flow thresholds or closure probability. Failing either test is reported as failed.

## Amendment 1 (2026-10-24, after reading the legend text of `bcso_FloodRoads_2018.pdf`, before any point was geocoded or matched)
The legend has 20 numbered lines of the form "N: Road name (SR number) at Feature [alternative name]". Fixed parse rules: the number in parentheses is the route number (`17` = US 17, `17-B` = US 17 Business, four-digit numbers = NC SR); text in square brackets is an alternative name of the same feature, and either name may be used to find it; "Feature" may be a stream, river, creek, swamp, pond, lake or retention pond. Three lines name something that is not a waterway (point 2 "State Line", point 3 "Hickman Rd", point 20 "Magnolia Greens Entrance"): they are located only if the named feature exists as an OSM road/boundary/entrance that the named road meets, and are otherwise reported **unlocated**; they are never given hand-picked coordinates. Ponds and lakes (points 13, 14, 19) are located as the road's crossing of the OSM waterbody or waterway of that name. No other rule changes: matching radius, sets, tests, seeds and pass criteria are as above.
